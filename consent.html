What this page does not establish

  • Location. The sweep records what fired from wherever it ran, and does not record where that was. A site with a geolocation rule behaves differently elsewhere.
  • One page. The homepage only. A tag that fires on a form page and not the homepage is invisible here.
  • A floor, not a total. Some vendors decline to fire under automation, so a tracker count is the least that fired, never the most.
  • Not a compliance verdict. These are observations about tag behaviour. Whether a configuration meets a given law is a question for someone qualified to answer it.
  • Cookieless Google pings are not leaks. A GA or Ads request carrying gcs=G100 after a rejection is what a correctly configured site does; it has its own column rather than a finding.
